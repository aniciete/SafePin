# Philippine Government Agency Logos

This directory contains official seals and logos of Philippine government agencies used in the SafePin platform.

## Usage Rights and Attribution

All government agency seals and logos in this directory are official emblems of the Republic of the Philippines and are used in accordance with Philippine law and regulations.

### Legal Basis for Usage

- **Republic Act No. 8491 (Flag and Heraldic Code of the Philippines)** - Governs the use of national symbols and government seals
- **Executive Order No. 292 (Administrative Code of 1987)** - Provides guidelines for government agency identification
- **Government Information Systems Plan** - Allows legitimate use of government logos for official business and public information

## Usage Guidelines

### Permitted Uses
- Official business correspondence with government agencies
- Public information and educational materials
- Incident reporting and emergency response coordination
- Platform identification of authorized government partners

### Prohibited Uses
- Commercial advertising or marketing
- Political campaigns or partisan activities
- Unauthorized modifications or alterations
- Misrepresentation of government endorsement

### Technical Specifications
- Format: SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics)
- Color: Original government-specified colors maintained
- Resolution: Vector format ensures scalability without quality loss
- Accessibility: Alt text and descriptions provided for screen readers

The use of these government seals and logos is strictly for the legitimate business purposes of SafePin as an incident reporting platform that coordinates with Philippine government agencies. This usage does not imply endorsement by the Philippine government of SafePin's services, nor does it suggest any official government affiliation beyond the legitimate business relationship for public safety coordination.
