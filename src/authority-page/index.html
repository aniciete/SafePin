SafePin Help Center

How do I manage reports?

As an authority user, you can review, verify, and manage reports submitted by residents in your jurisdiction. Use the Reports section to view and update report statuses.

How are reports verified?

Review each report's details, evidence, and location. Update the verification status based on your assessment and add official notes as needed.

What are the report statuses?

Reports can be marked as: Pending Verification, Verified, Resolved, or Rejected. Each status helps track the progress of incident management.

Who can access the data?

Access to incident data is role-based. You can only view and manage reports within your assigned jurisdiction.
